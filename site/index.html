01 / The NatureCam project

From watching wildlife
to understanding it.

NatureCam is Xeroth’s project to connect nature cameras, human expertise and AI. Djuma is where we’re starting—with the people who know this camera best.

01 — OBSERVE

A window that stays open

Nature cameras let people return to the same place, day after day. Those patient observations reveal behaviours and patterns a single visit can miss.

Watch Djuma Cam ↗
02 — RECOGNISE

Hosana: who came back?

Hosana is the re-identification pipeline we’re building. Its goal is to connect repeat sightings of the same animal over time, starting at Djuma. Individual recognition is still being researched and validated.

In development
03 — ASSIST

An AI companion for zoomies

Zoomies are the people who remotely pan, tilt and zoom nature cameras. We’re developing an AI zoomie to help them notice activity, follow animals and choose useful views, with human judgement at the centre.

In development
1998

Djuma’s first webcam began sending still images in August 1998. Later that year it moved to overlook Gowrie Dam. A community of viewers and remote camera operators grew around this enduring window into the South African bush. Read Djuma’s history ↗

02 / Open research

Can a voice tell us
who’s calling?

Recognising a species is one thing. Recognising an individual animal is much harder.

Our acoustic re-identification research asks whether AI learns an animal’s voice—or shortcuts such as the microphone, background or recording session. We tested these questions across nine species.

03 / Listen in at Djuma

A soundscape, made visible.

With BirdNET-Cloud, we’re identifying likely bird species in calls from Djuma Cam. Explore the latest detections, browse species and follow each call’s record. Open to everyone, with no login.

Automated species suggestions—not confirmed individual identities.
