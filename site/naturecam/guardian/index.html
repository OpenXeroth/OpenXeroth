OpenXeroth / NatureCam / Guardian

Guardian

Keep the broadcast going with a standby video when the live camera feed drops.

Give viewers something to see when the camera feed drops

A camera or its connection can fail for a few seconds or longer. Guardian watches the incoming picture and sound. If the feed stops or becomes too unreliable to use, it switches to a prepared standby video. When the live feed has recovered and Guardian has a clean picture to start from, it switches back.

Switch without decoding or re-encoding the video

The camera and the standby video are already compressed. Guardian takes the ready-to-send video and sound from the selected source and lines up their timing so the outgoing broadcast can continue. It does not turn the video back into pixels or compress it again. That saves processing power and avoids the loss of picture quality that another compression pass can introduce.

Keep the roles separate

Guardian handles interruptions in the incoming camera feed. Restreamer takes Guardian’s output and sends it to YouTube. Sentinel records the camera feed separately, so the standby video stays out of the sighting archive.
