OpenXeroth / NatureCam / Zoomie dashboard

Zoomie dashboard

Let volunteer operators steer the camera without logging in to the camera itself.

A control room in the browser

Djuma’s volunteers, known as zoomies, take turns looking for wildlife and following it as it moves. The dashboard gives approved operators a live view and controls to pan, tilt and zoom from a browser. The picture has a short delay so an operator can see the effect of a move and stop where they want.

Why separate the dashboard from the camera?

Operators sign in to NatureCam with their own accounts. The dashboard sends their moves to the camera, so volunteers do not need the camera’s password or access to its settings. NatureCam gives camera control to one operator at a time so two people cannot steer against each other.

Viewers can follow the same camera on YouTube while a zoomie is at the controls. Open the Djuma dashboard or watch the broadcast.
