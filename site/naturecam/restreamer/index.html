OpenXeroth / NatureCam / Restreamer

Restreamer

Deliver the broadcast to YouTube and give operators a way to check and switch its feeds.

The handoff to YouTube

Restreamer takes the video and sound from Guardian and sends them to Djuma’s public and members broadcasts. We use the datarhei Restreamer project for this job. It gives operators a preview and a way to check whether each outgoing feed is running.

Give YouTube a feed it can play reliably

Sound and picture arrive as a series of small pieces. YouTube needs to know when to play each piece and how to read the picture. Restreamer forwards the existing video and sound without compressing them again. We have tuned the handoff to preserve sound timing and pass along the information YouTube needs to read the video when a source changes.

Backup feeds when needed

Each broadcast has a backup feed that can take over during work on Guardian. The two backup senders normally stay off. The public and members broadcasts each use one outgoing stream, so standby feeds do not use extra internet upload capacity. The backups still depend on the same camera and Restreamer, so they cannot cover an outage of either.
