OpenXeroth / NatureCam
Born at Djuma, South Africa

Software for live wildlife cameras

NatureCam helps people operate a wildlife camera from anywhere, keep its live broadcast running and save footage of animal sightings.

NatureCam began at Djuma Cam, which looks out over Gowrie Dam in the Sabi Sand. Djuma is the only camera NatureCam operates today. The aim is to make the software available to other nature camera teams so they can run their own broadcasts and build records of wildlife where they are.

What runs Djuma Cam

Zoomie dashboard

Let volunteer operators steer the camera without logging in to the camera itself.

Restreamer

Deliver the broadcast to YouTube and give operators a way to check and switch its feeds.

Sentinel

Save original camera footage of wildlife sightings for later review and research.

Guardian

Keep the broadcast going with a standby video when the live camera feed drops.

From live viewing to research

A live camera lets people share a view of the waterhole. Saving the footage lets researchers return to a sighting, compare what happened on different days and test tools that could help operators find and follow animals. Explore the research.
