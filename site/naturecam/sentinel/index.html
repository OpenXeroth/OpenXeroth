OpenXeroth / NatureCam / Sentinel

Sentinel

Save original camera footage of wildlife sightings for later review and research.

Keep the sighting after it has passed

An animal can cross the waterhole and disappear in seconds. Sentinel continually records the camera feed in short clips. It examines frames for signs of wildlife and saves relevant clips, including footage around a detection that may show how an animal arrived or left.

Keep the camera’s original footage

Sentinel records before Guardian can switch to a standby video and before Restreamer sends anything to YouTube. It keeps the video the camera sent, without re-encoding it or recording from a YouTube player. If the live picture is lost, the recording has a gap instead of showing the standby video as though it were a sighting.

Make footage useful beyond the live stream

Saved clips can be replayed and checked frame by frame. Sentinel keeps information about when frames were captured and where the camera was pointing, when those measurements are available. Researchers can also set a recording window that retains all footage received during that time, including clips in which the detector finds nothing. A detection is a prompt to look at the footage; it does not identify an individual animal.
